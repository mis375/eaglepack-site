Privacy Policy

Eagle Thermo Packaging (Eagle Pack) · Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how Eagle Thermo Packaging ("we", "us") handles information when you message "Isha", our virtual assistant on WhatsApp. Isha answers questions about our products and services (including Ezeepack and BinWin) and passes your request to our team when a person needs to help.

1. Information we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name shown on WhatsApp.
  • The messages you send to Isha, including text and voice notes, and the replies Isha sends back.
  • Details you choose to share while asking for a quote or help, such as your company name, product requirement, quantity or delivery city.

We do not ask you for passwords, card numbers or bank details on WhatsApp, and you should not send them.

2. How we use it

  • To answer your questions and prepare quotes or product information.
  • To hand your conversation to a member of our team when needed.
  • To keep a record of enquiries, check the quality of Isha's answers and fix mistakes.

We do not sell your information.

3. Service providers who process your messages

To run Isha we use the following providers, who process messages only to provide their service to us:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Anthropic to generate Isha's replies.
  • OpenAI to convert voice notes into text.
  • Google (Google Sheets) to keep our enquiry log.
  • Cloudflare to host the software that connects these services.

4. How long we keep it

We keep conversation records only for as long as they are needed to serve your enquiry, maintain business records and improve the quality of the service. You can ask us to delete them at any time (see section 6).

5. Your choices

You may stop messaging Isha at any time. You may ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.

6. Data deletion instructions

To delete your data, send an email to mis@eaglepack.in with the subject "Delete my data" from any address, and mention the WhatsApp number you used to message Isha. We will delete the conversation records linked to that number and confirm by reply. You can also message "delete my data" to Isha on WhatsApp and our team will follow up.

7. Security

We limit access to enquiry records to authorised team members and keep access keys for our systems private. No online service is completely risk free, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.
