What we make

Packs shaped around your product.

We form clear and coloured sheet into packaging that holds, protects and presents. Most work is made to order against your sample or drawing.

  • Blister and card-seal packs
  • Hinged clamshells
  • Cavity and insert trays
  • Sliding blister packs
  • Material-handling trays
Materials

Sheet materials, many uses

A-PET

Clear, glossy sheet used where the product should be seen. Common for retail and food-contact style packs.

HIPS

Rigid, opaque sheet suited to trays and inserts that carry components.

PVC

Clear, rigid sheet widely used for blister packs and clamshells.

R-PET

PET sheet made from recycled material. Ask us about availability for your pack.

Suitability for a specific use, including food contact and thickness range, is confirmed with you at quote stage.
