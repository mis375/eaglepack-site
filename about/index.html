Home / About us

A family business, built in Noida since 1999.

Anil Arora started Eagle Pack in 1999. Today the group runs two divisions from one address, led by the next generation of the family.

Our story

From one founder to two divisions.

Eagle Pack began in 1999 under Anil Arora, now Founder and Chairman. Over the years the work grew from packaging for industry into clear packaging for food, gifting and retail, and into storage bins.

Today the group has two divisions, each a separate company: Eagle Thermo Packaging and Eagle Solutions. BinWin sits inside Eagle Thermo Packaging, and Ezeepack sits inside Eagle Solutions.

How the group fits together

Two divisions, two brands, one group

Division 01

Eagle Thermo Packaging

Thermoformed packaging made to order, plus the BinWin bin range.

Division 02

Eagle Solutions

Clear tins, jars, boxes and supplies. Sold to retail customers under the Ezeepack brand.

Brands

BinWin and Ezeepack

BinWin is the clear PVC bin range of Eagle Thermo Packaging. Ezeepack is the retail brand of Eagle Solutions.
